## Functional Requirements

### FR-001: Brand-order narrative sequencing

The system shall establish Maison Muelhens as the narrative sender before Cologne Culture
Cosmetics is introduced, on every page and at every scroll depth, framing CCC always as "the
first brand"/"first chapter" of the house, never as a standalone product brand appearing before
or independent of the house narrative. [EARS: ubiquitous]

### FR-002: Classic 3-scene hero slider (replaces cinematic hero)

When a visitor advances past slide 3 or goes back past slide 1, the system shall loop the hero
slider infinitely (advancing past slide 3 returns to slide 1, going back past slide 1 wraps to
slide 3), matching the reference mockup's numbered slide indicators (01/02/03) and left/right
arrow navigation, with neither arrow ever disabled or dead-ended. [EARS: event-driven]
